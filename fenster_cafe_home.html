The Fenster Story

More than just a place for coffee, Fenster Cafe is a space to slow down.

Located on the 2nd Floor of Sunday Hub, Katargam, Fenster was conceived as a tactile sanctuary amidst Surat's energetic tempo. The name ‘Fenster’—German for window—reflects our belief that a cafe should offer an opening into warmth, calm, and connection.

From precise pour-over extraction to naturally fermented sourdough, every element that reaches your table is treated with honest culinary craft. Whether you are opening a favorite book, hosting a relaxed work meeting, or sharing wood-fired plates with close friends, our doors are open daily until midnight.
